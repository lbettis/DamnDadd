Who we are

Damn Dadd is the Bettis Family. What started as a dad with a camera became a home for our adventures: riding the Lake Superior Circle Tour on a Harley, flying drones through the morning fog, snow tubing on New Year's Day and visiting The Gambia.

Now we're building things for families like ours. God First, Money Wise helps families save together, and Black Movies Trivia celebrates more than a century of Black film.
